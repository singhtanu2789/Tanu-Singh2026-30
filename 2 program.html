Btech CS
BTech CSE, or Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering,
is a four-year undergraduate course divided into eight semesters.
It provides students with both theoretical and practical knowledge of computer systems,
programming languages, algorithms, data structures,
operating systems, computer networks, databases, software engineering, and emerging technologies like artificial intelligence, machine learning, and cybersecurity to know more click here
Btech AIML
Bachelor of Technology in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning,
is a four-year undergraduate course divided into eight semesters.
It provides students with both theoretical and practical knowledge of AI and ML concepts, algorithms, data analysis, and applications.
Btech DS
Bachelor of Technology in Data Science,
is a four-year undergraduate course divided into eight semesters.
It provides students with both theoretical and practical knowledge of data analysis, statistics, and machine learning.
Btech COE
Bachelor of Technology in Computer Engineering,
is a four-year undergraduate course divided into eight semesters.
It provides students with both theoretical and practical knowledge of computer hardware, software, and systems.
